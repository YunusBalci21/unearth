      How to play
      Field guide
      
            
        
      
        
      
            
      
                
      
                
      1 · Excavate
      Every site is an 8×8 grid of excavation units with three layers of soil above bedrock. Click a unit to remove a layer. Deeper layers are older — and hold the rarest finds.
      
            
      
            
      
                
      
                
      2 · Read the clues
      Potsherds on the surface lie near buried finds. Dark soil stains mark something deep below. A survey probe counts every hidden find in the 3×3 units around it.
      
            
      
            
      
                
      
                
      3 · Recover
      When a find starts to show through the soil, brush it clean to lift it out. Shovels are fast but can chip what they uncover — badly damaged finds can’t be identified.
      
            
      
            
      
                
      
                
      4 · Identify the site
      Use your finds to guess the country. One find or none: 500 points, then 100 less per extra find — plus a second-by-second time bonus. A wrong answer costs 100.
      
            
      
        
      

        
      
            
      ToolKeyUse it for
      
            
                
      Shovel1Clearing a 3×3 area quickly. Risky near finds.
      
                
      Trowel2Careful work on a single unit. Never damages a find.
      
                
      Brush3Cleaning an exposed find until it can be lifted out.
      
                
      Probe4Counting the finds in a 3×3 area. Three per site.
      
                  
        
      
        
      Right-drag (or one-finger drag) to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom, R to reset the view. Arrow keys move the selected unit and Enter digs.
      

        
      
            
      About Unearth
      
            
      Unearth is a free archaeology guessing game for your browser — part excavation, part geography quiz. Its archive holds 256 artifacts from 32 countries, from Egyptian funerary masks and Chinese oracle bones to Viking swords, Benin brasses and 2.6-million-year-old stone tools, each with a short note on its real history.
      
            
      Play a solo expedition worldwide or by region, take on the Daily Dig that everyone plays with the same sites, or host a private lobby and race up to nine friends to identify each site. Every artifact you recover is catalogued in your personal Archive.
